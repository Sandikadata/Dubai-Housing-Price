# # Dubai Housing Price Analysis | Power BI Dashboard

## Project Overview
An interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing **50,000 property listings in Dubai** to explore housing prices, property sizes, bedroom configurations, property age, listing categories, and neighborhood distribution.

The project helps buyers, investors, and real estate agents identify suitable properties and understand market trends through interactive visualizations and filters.

## Key Market Insights
- **Mid-range properties:** Account for 65.45% of total listings.
- **High-end properties:** Represent 25.99% of listings.
- **Budget properties:** Account for 8.57% of listings.
- **Neighborhoods:** Urban properties contribute approximately 33.55% of the market's price share.
- **Property size:** Larger properties generally show higher prices.
- **Bedrooms:** Three- and four-bedroom properties are highlighted as popular family-home segments.
- **Property age:** Properties aged 40–70 years show the highest total price in the analyzed dataset.

## Dashboard Features
- Interactive filters for square feet, bedrooms, bathrooms, listing category, neighborhood, and property age.
- Price and property-size analysis.
- Average price comparison by listing category and neighborhood.
- Property distribution analysis by size, age, and price.
- Insights for residential buyers, investment buyers, and real estate agents.

## Business Recommendations
- Prioritize mid-range properties for broader buyer demand.
- Focus on three- and four-bedroom homes for family buyers.
- Evaluate urban properties for premium investment opportunities.
- Explore budget properties for entry-level buyers.
- Assess renovated older properties and larger homes for potential investment value.
- Diversify investments across urban, suburban, and rural neighborhoods.

## Business Value
This dashboard transforms a large property dataset into actionable insights, helping real estate professionals filter listings efficiently, compare property options, understand market segments, and support informed business decisions.

*Note: Market findings describe the analyzed dataset and should not be interpreted as independently verified market-wide demand, sales speed, or investment returns.*
